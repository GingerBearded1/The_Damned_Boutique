Our Story

A little dark.
A little weird.
Entirely yours.

Tucked into the heart of Boulder City, The Damned Boutique is where the alternative, the curious, and the unapologetically weird come to feel at home.

From hand-poured candles and crystals to alt clothing, books, and one-of-a-kind gifts — everything here was chosen with intention.
